# Publisher Outreach Email — Template

> Purpose: notify each publisher that SLNews includes their headlines/summaries/links, and open the door to a proper licensing/partnership conversation. This is a **goodwill + partnership** email — we already operate in link+summary mode (which needs no permission), so there's no ask to "approve scraping"; the ask is: *keep you informed, give credit, and explore working together*.
>
> Fill the `{placeholders}` and send one per publisher. Keep it short — editors are busy.

---

## Subject options

- `SLNews: we feature {Publisher Name} headlines — let's talk partnership`
- `Partnership inquiry from SLNews (Sierra Leone news platform)`

## Email body

Hi {Editor name / "Editorial Team"},

I'm {Your name}, building **SLNews** — a free Sierra Leone news platform (https://sl-news.vercel.app) that brings local journalism together in one feed: national news, market prices, and community notices.

We currently include headlines, short summaries, and links to articles from **{Publisher Name}**, always credited back to your site. Our goal is simple: **drive more readers to your reporting** while making Sierra Leone news easier to find.

Two things I'd like to do:

1. **Confirm you're comfortable** with us listing your headlines + summaries + a link back to your site (the standard "link + summary" model used by Google News and similar aggregators). If you'd prefer we not include {Publisher Name}, we'll remove it immediately.

2. **Explore a proper partnership.** We're building a news-data API that serves these headlines/summaries to developers and media clients. If you're open to it, we'd love to discuss:
   - a **revenue share** for any paid API usage of your content, and/or
   - a **promotional partnership** (featured placement for {Publisher Name} on the platform).

No obligation at all — this is just to keep you in the loop and see if there's a fit. Happy to hop on a call or answer questions by email.

Best regards,
{Your name}
{Your title / role}
SLNews — {your email} | {your phone (optional)}

## Notes before sending

- Find the right contact: look for an **About/Contact** page on each site; "editor@…" or a contact form is fine. Personalise the first line if you know the editor's name.
- Send from a real address (`{your email}`), sign with your full name — it reads much better than an anonymous account.
- If a publisher replies "no": thank them and add them to the API blocklist (stop ingesting) — keep the relationship friendly.
- If they reply "yes but how?" — the answer is: we only ever show ~1–2 sentence summaries plus your link; full articles are never republished. Offer to share a sample of exactly what we display.
- If they're interested in revenue share: that's Phase 3 territory — acknowledge interest and say you'll follow up with specifics (don't promise numbers in this first email).
